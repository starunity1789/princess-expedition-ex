[h1]The Princesses' Expedition EX[/h1]

The Emperor stays behind on a divided homeworld while his four daughters take the mobile fortress "Origin Fortress" to the stars. Their people fall to an incurable illness and can no longer bear children, so the princesses lead them down the path of synthetic fertility.

Setting: [url=https://github.com/starunity1789/StargazerPR]The Stargazer Project[/url]. [b]Requires a new game.[/b]

[h2]Origin: The Princesses' Expedition[/h2]
[list]
[*] Every mechanic of vanilla [b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]; Synthetic Ascension and Dictatorial only
[*] Capital is a size-25 mobile fortress; the [b]four princesses[/b] serve as Ruler and Councilors - immortal, cannot be dismissed
[*] Starts in its own cluster beyond the rim at 12 o'clock, with an L-Gate and a Ruined Mega Shipyard in the home system
[*] Bundled name list and preset empire [b]Empire of AllLife Chance-Born[/b], ready on the empire selection screen
[/list]

[h2]Galaxy[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Five guaranteed great powers[/b]: Burning White Stag Legion, Far Star Alliance, Grand Suzerain Allied Fief, and the genocidal Tide of Dread and Terminal Alignment Protocol
[*] The Chosen and the Keides crash site always spawn; Mega Shipyards have 50 slots; Galactic Community sessions and recesses take half as long
[*] Vanilla shields, armor and non-bio ship hull doubled; +100K storage
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
Stellaris 4.5.*; The Machine Age and Ancient Relics (for the origin); the great powers and events also use Distant Stars, First Contact, Synthetic Dawn, MegaCorp, Astral Planes, Necroids, Nemesis and BioGenesis. Bundles the Ancient Dragonkin and Kemonomimi portrait packs - [b]disable those if you are subscribed[/b]. Fully overrides several vanilla files, so it may conflict with mods that change the same files.
