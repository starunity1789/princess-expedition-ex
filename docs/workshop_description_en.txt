[h1]The Princesses' Expedition EX[/h1]

The homeworld stayed divided, so the Emperor remained behind and his four daughters took the mobile fortress "Origin Fortress" to the distant Origin's Reach to found the Empire anew. Their colonists fell to an incurable illness and can no longer bear children - so the princesses lead their people down the path of synthetic fertility.

Setting: [url=https://github.com/starunity1789/StargazerPR]The Stargazer Project[/url]. [b]Requires a new game.[/b]

[h2]Origin: The Princesses' Expedition[/h2]
[list]
[*] Includes every mechanic of vanilla [b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]; Synthetic Ascension and Dictatorial only
[*] Capital is the [b]Origin Fortress[/b], a size-25 mobile habitat at 100% habitability
[*] The [b]four princesses[/b] start as Ruler and Councilors with custom portraits - immortal, no upkeep, cannot be dismissed
[*] Relic [b]Seal of the Princess Guard[/b], the [b]Dreadnoughts[/b] tech and two unique decisions
[*] The L-Cluster always ends with the Gray Tempest, but Gray can always be found
[/list]

[h2]New in EX[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Rim cluster[/b]: the expedition moves to 7 systems beyond the rim at 2 o'clock, joined to the galaxy by a single hyperlane
[*] [b]Three guaranteed great powers[/b]: Burning White Stag Legion (12 o'clock), Far Star Alliance (5 o'clock) and Grand Suzerain Allied Fief (9 o'clock), each with a fixed flag, a named ruler and a monthly supply
[*] [b]Galaxy rules[/b]: The Chosen and the Keides crash site always spawn; +100K storage; non-bio ship hull doubled; [b]every vanilla shield and armor value doubled[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Required DLC[/h2]
The Machine Age and Ancient Relics (for the origin); Distant Stars, First Contact, Synthetic Dawn, MegaCorp, Astral Planes, Necroids, Nemesis; Galactic Paragons recommended.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Stellaris 4.5.*
[*] Bundles the Ancient Dragonkin and Kemonomimi portrait packs - [b]disable those if you are subscribed[/b]
[*] Fully overrides vanilla 00_authorities.txt, 00_living_standards.txt, 00_portrait_sets.txt and 02_scripted_variables_component_cost.txt, plus the game rule can_dismiss_leader and the council-portrait hologram shader
[/list]
