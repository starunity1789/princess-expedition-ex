[h1]The Princesses' Expedition EX[/h1]

The homeworld stayed divided, so the Emperor remained behind and his four daughters took the mobile fortress "Origin Fortress" to the distant Origin's Reach to found the Empire anew. Their colonists fell to an incurable illness and can no longer bear children - so the princesses lead their people down the path of synthetic fertility.

Setting: [url=https://github.com/starunity1789/StargazerPR]The Stargazer Project[/url]. [b]Requires a new game.[/b]

[h2]Origin: The Princesses' Expedition[/h2]
[list]
[*] Includes every mechanic of vanilla [b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]; Synthetic Ascension only, Dictatorial only, one per galaxy
[*] Capital is the [b]Origin Fortress[/b], a size-25 mobile habitat at 100% habitability with 15 each of Housing / Generator / Mining / Agriculture districts
[*] The [b]four princesses[/b] start as Ruler and Councilors with custom portraits - immortal, no upkeep, cannot be dismissed, each with her own destiny trait
[*] Relic [b]Seal of the Princess Guard[/b]: 3000 energy summons 5 permanent guard warforms; +2 minor artifacts per month
[*] Decisions "Establish the Expeditionary Order" and "Explore the Secrets of the Precursors", plus the Dreadnoughts tech
[*] The L-Cluster always ends with the Gray Tempest, but Gray can always be found
[/list]

[h2]EX: the Rim Cluster[/h2]
At game start the whole expedition moves to 7 systems beyond the rim at 2 o'clock, joined to the galaxy by a single hyperlane: an L-Gate, a pre-FTL civilization (always on the Ancient Dragonkin portraits), a black hole, a neutron star, a pulsar, and a Relic World carrying the vanilla "Planet-Wide Machinery" anomaly.

[h2]EX: Three Guaranteed Great Powers[/h2]
Always spawned, each in its own cluster beyond the rim with two hyperlanes into the galaxy, each with a fixed empire flag.
[list]
[*] [b]Burning White Stag Legion[/b] (12 o'clock) machine empire on the Kemonomimi portraits; ruler "Fused-Heart Calculus" with a custom portrait
[*] [b]Far Star Alliance[/b] (5 o'clock) authoritarian state; ruler "Taiaria Starheart" with a custom portrait and 5 traits
[*] [b]Grand Suzerain Allied Fief[/b] (9 o'clock) necroid monarchy; its 12th ascension perk is always Galactic Nemesis
[/list]
Fixed monthly supply: 222 of each basic resource, 22 of each strategic resource, 2 influence, 45-56 of each research type. Once a Fallen Empire awakens or an endgame crisis begins it doubles, with another 550 of each research type.

[h2]EX: Galaxy Rules[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]The Chosen[/b] and the crash site of [b]Keides, Scion of Vagros[/b] always spawn
[*] All empires: resource storage +100K; non-bio ships have their base hull doubled
[*] [b]Every vanilla shield and armor value is doubled[/b] (Psi Shields, Dark Matter Deflectors and Dragonscale Armor included)
[/list]

[h2]Required DLC[/h2]
The Machine Age and Ancient Relics (for the origin); Distant Stars, First Contact, Synthetic Dawn, MegaCorp, Astral Planes, Necroids, Nemesis; Galactic Paragons recommended.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Stellaris 4.5.*
[*] Bundles the Ancient Dragonkin and Kemonomimi portrait packs - [b]disable those if you are subscribed[/b]
[*] Fully overrides vanilla 00_authorities.txt, 00_living_standards.txt and 02_scripted_variables_component_cost.txt, plus the game rule can_dismiss_leader and the council-portrait hologram shader
